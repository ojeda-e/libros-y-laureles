Welcome to Books and Laurels.


We are friends who have had an ongoing book club running starting in 2026. Each month, we pick a theme and each of us chooses a book within that theme for us to read in our free time. In this website, we post a joint review of each book we read for our book club. 

The reviews included in this website are our own opinions, thoughts or perspectives, and not necessarily the opinions of the authors or publishers. **We encourage you to read the book and form your own opinion.**
